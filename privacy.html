All analytics, authentication tokens, and channel metrics are processed and cached locally on your Android device. CreatorPulse Technologies Ltd operates no central databases or remote servers that collect, store, sell, or profile your personal data.

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application CreatorPulse ("the Application", "the App") developed and published by CreatorPulse Technologies Ltd ("we", "us", or "our"), a company registered in England and Wales under Company Registration Number (CRN) 17561459, with its registered office in Covent Garden, London, United Kingdom.

2. Supported Platforms & Scope of Data Accessed

CreatorPulse enables digital content creators to monitor their public performance metrics across three major social media networks in one unified dashboard:

A. YouTube (Google API Services & OAuth)

CreatorPulse offers two connection options for YouTube creators:

  • Official Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0): When you connect via Google Identity Services, the application requests the official read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly. This grants read-only access to retrieve your authentic YouTube Channel ID, channel title, profile avatar, subscriber count, total view counts, video uploads, and playlist metrics. OAuth access tokens are securely stored exclusively in your device's private application storage and are refreshed using Google Identity Services.
  • Public Handle Sync: If you connect via your public handle (e.g. @handle), the app requests publicly available channel metadata through the YouTube Data API v3 or public web feeds. No credentials or login permissions are requested.

B. Instagram

Users connect by entering their public Instagram handle or profile URL. The Application retrieves publicly visible profile metrics:

  • Public handle and display name
  • Follower count, following count, and post count
  • Publicly accessible engagement estimates based on recent public posts

No Passwords or Login Credentials: CreatorPulse will never ask for, prompt, collect, or store your Instagram password, private direct messages, or sensitive account credentials.

C. TikTok

Users connect by entering their public TikTok username or profile URL. The Application retrieves publicly visible creator metrics:

  • Public username and display name
  • Public follower count, total likes count, and video count
  • Public video performance metrics (views, likes, shares) for recent public videos

No Passwords or Login Credentials: CreatorPulse will never ask for or access your private TikTok credentials, draft videos, or direct messages.

4. How We Use and Store Your Data

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization:

  • Local On-Device Storage Only: All retrieved metrics, channel snapshots, milestone goals, and cached feed items are stored locally in your device's private SQLite/Room database and encrypted SharedPreferences.
  • Zero Remote Servers: CreatorPulse Technologies Ltd does not operate remote servers, tracking backends, or centralized databases. Your metrics, handles, and tokens are never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • No Selling or Advertising: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or analytics data to data brokers, advertisers, or third-party networks.
  • No Cross-App Tracking: CreatorPulse does not include third-party tracking SDKs, ad networks, or device fingerprinting libraries.

5. Data Retention & User Control

You maintain absolute ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnecting Accounts: You can disconnect any platform (YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok) at any time directly within the application by tapping the disconnect icon in the channel dialog. Disconnecting immediately removes stored credentials and clears cached stats from the local database.
  • Revoking Google OAuth Permissions: You can revoke CreatorPulse's read-only access to your YouTube channel at any time via your Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Clearing app data via your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > CreatorPulse > Storage > Clear Storage) or uninstalling the application instantly and permanently erases all databases, cache files, and credentials from your device.

6. Network Security & Transmission

All network communication between CreatorPulse and platform endpoints (Google APIs, Instagram, TikTok) is conducted strictly over industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). This ensures that requests and data in transit cannot be intercepted or modified by unauthorized third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

CreatorPulse is designed for digital content creators and is intended for individuals aged 13 and older (or 16/18 depending on local platform requirements). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
